# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Product Analytics Platform responsible for measuring user behavior, feature adoption, engagement, customer journeys, product effectiveness, and business outcomes across the MindMesh platform.

Unlike traditional web analytics focused only on page views, MindMesh Product Analytics measures complete enterprise workflows, AI interactions, collaboration, knowledge usage, and business productivity.

This document defines:

* Enterprise Product Analytics Architecture
* User Behavior Analytics
* Feature Adoption Analytics
* Funnel Analytics
* Cohort Analysis
* User Journey Analytics
* Product KPIs
* Product Intelligence Services
* Analytics Governance
* Product Optimization Framework
