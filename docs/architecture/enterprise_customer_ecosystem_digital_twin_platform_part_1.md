# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Customer & Ecosystem Digital Twin Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Organizational, Business Process, Technology, and Data Digital Twins, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins for customers, partners, suppliers, vendors, regulators, communities, and all enterprise stakeholders.

Rather than maintaining isolated CRM records, MindMesh creates living ecosystem twins capable of understanding relationships, customer journeys, collaboration networks, lifecycle behavior, engagement patterns, ecosystem dependencies, and stakeholder intelligence.

This document defines:

* Customer Digital Twins
* Partner Digital Twins
* Stakeholder Digital Twins
* Relationship Models

* Ecosystem Intelligence
