# Purpose

This document establishes the architectural foundation for the AI-native intelligence platform that powers MindMesh.

Unlike traditional enterprise software that merely embeds AI features, MindMesh is designed as an **AI-first Knowledge Intelligence Platform**, where intelligence is a native platform capability.

This document introduces the overall architecture that governs every AI component, knowledge service, reasoning engine, retrieval system, autonomous agent, enterprise memory, and intelligence workflow.

This phase serves as the architectural blueprint for all subsequent AI architecture documents.

---
