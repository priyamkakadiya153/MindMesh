# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Organizational Operating Model (EOM) and Business Operating System (BOS) for MindMesh by defining how the organization operates, collaborates, delivers customer value, governs execution, and aligns strategy with business capabilities, domains, products, engineering, AI, and operations.

While previous documents defined **what the enterprise does (Capabilities)**, **how software is organized (Domains)**, **how systems collaborate (Context Mapping)**, **how work executes (Business Processes)**, and **how decisions are made (Decision Intelligence)**, this document defines **how the enterprise itself operates.**

This document defines:

* Enterprise Operating Model
* Organizational Structure
* Business Functions
* Operating Capabilities
* Enterprise Services
* Operating Principles
* Organizational Roles
* Cross-Functional Collaboration
* Business Execution Model
* Enterprise Operating Framework
