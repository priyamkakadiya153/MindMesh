# Purpose

This document defines the **Enterprise Cognitive Knowledge Graph (ECKG)** and the **Universal Semantic Fabric (USF)**, the semantic foundation of the MindMesh Enterprise Cognitive Operating System (ECOS).

The Enterprise Cognitive Knowledge Graph transforms isolated enterprise data into a continuously evolving network of interconnected knowledge, enabling AI agents, executive copilots, applications, digital twins, workflows, and humans to understand enterprise meaning rather than merely processing data.

The Universal Semantic Fabric establishes a shared understanding of every enterprise entity, relationship, process, event, capability, policy, and decision.

This architecture becomes the **semantic brain** of MindMesh.

To ensure compliance with the [MindMesh Engineering Constitution](file:///d:/7%20sem/MindMesh/.agents/AGENTS.md):
* **Knowledge First Principle**: Meaning and structure of knowledge assets must be prioritized over conversational logging.
* **Heuristic Offline Fallback**: In the absence of online LLM/AI services, the knowledge graph shifts to deterministic pattern matching, semantic lookup indices, and local tokenizers to maintain query routing.
* **Security & Tenancy**: Semantic relationships enforce tenant isolation, ensuring a tenant's knowledge graph partition cannot leak metadata or node information to another tenant under any circumstance.
* **Trace Auditing**: Each added node, edge, or inferred relationship undergoes source verification, provenance logging, and policy compliance auditing.
