# Purpose

This document expands the Enterprise Business Capability Architecture by defining the enterprise capability map, capability taxonomy, strategic business capabilities, business value streams, domain ownership, and enterprise operating model.

The Enterprise Capability Map provides the highest-level representation of what MindMesh does as an organization.

Unlike organizational charts or software architecture, capability maps remain stable despite organizational restructuring or technology changes.

This document defines:

* Enterprise Capability Model
* Capability Taxonomy
* Strategic Business Capabilities
* Business Value Streams
* Domain Mapping
* Capability Ownership
* Capability Portfolio
* Enterprise Operating Model
* Capability Heat Maps
* Strategy Alignment

---

# Vision

Every investment, product, platform, AI system, and engineering initiative should trace directly to one or more enterprise business capabilities.
