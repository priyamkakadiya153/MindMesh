# Purpose

This document defines the **MindMesh Organizational Architecture**, establishing how the company attracts, develops, manages, and retains talent while continuously evolving its workforce.

The framework integrates organizational design, workforce planning, recruitment, performance management, learning, leadership development, employee experience, AI-assisted HR, and organizational intelligence.

People become the most valuable enterprise asset.
