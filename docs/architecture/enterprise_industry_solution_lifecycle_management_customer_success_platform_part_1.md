# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Industry Solution Lifecycle Management & Customer Success Platform** for MindMesh.

While previous documents defined Industry Intelligence, Domain AI, Regulatory Intelligence, Industry Accelerators, Analytics, Multi-Tenant Architecture, and Enterprise Integrations, this document defines how enterprise customers successfully adopt, deploy, operate, and continuously realize value from MindMesh throughout the entire solution lifecycle.

Rather than viewing deployment as the end of implementation, MindMesh treats customer success as a continuous lifecycle that spans onboarding, adoption, operational readiness, value realization, optimization, expansion, and renewal.

This document defines:

* Enterprise Solution Lifecycle
* Customer Onboarding
* Adoption Framework
* Customer Success Architecture
* Industry Implementation Framework
* Operational Readiness
* Customer Enablement
* Success Metrics
* Value Realization
* Lifecycle Governance
