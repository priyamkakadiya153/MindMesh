# Purpose

This document defines the **MindMesh Customer Experience Platform**, establishing the complete ecosystem through which customers, developers, partners, administrators, students, and enterprise users interact with MindMesh throughout their lifecycle.

It unifies customer onboarding, documentation, learning, developer experience, community collaboration, certification, events, support, customer advocacy, and product engagement into one integrated platform.

The Customer Experience Platform becomes the **engagement layer** of the MindMesh ecosystem.

To comply with the [MindMesh Engineering Constitution](file:///d:/7%20sem/MindMesh/.agents/AGENTS.md):
* **Tenant Isolation**: Customer experience databases, community forums, ticket management datasets, and training progression metrics enforce tenant boundaries. User profiles and training track completions cannot leak across tenants.
* **Resilient Outage Handling**: Academy and documentation platforms degrade gracefully. If remote LLMs or the interactive training sandbox is offline, documentation shifts to static static guides, local markdown guides, and search indexes.
* **Audit Tracing & Lineage**: Key customer operations (e.g., ticket creation, community moderator logs, academy certifications, support escalations) write records to trace lineage and verify compliance.
