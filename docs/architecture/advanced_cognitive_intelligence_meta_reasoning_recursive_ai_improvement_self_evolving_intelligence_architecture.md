# Purpose

This document explores long-term research into AI systems capable of improving how they reason, plan, learn, and collaborate within well-defined governance boundaries.

Rather than assuming unconstrained self-improvement, the focus is on **AI-assisted optimization under human oversight**, where improvements are evaluated, validated, and approved before deployment.

---

# Vision

Develop enterprise AI systems that become progressively better at reasoning, planning, and learning through evidence-based improvement while remaining transparent, governed, and aligned with organizational objectives.
