# Purpose

This document presents a long-term research vision for a **Universal Knowledge Network (UKN)**—a federated ecosystem in which organizations can interoperate through shared standards, semantic interoperability, and governed knowledge exchange.

Rather than proposing a single centralized repository of global knowledge, the vision explores interoperable networks of independently governed knowledge systems connected through common semantic models and open standards.

---

# Vision

Enable organizations to collaborate through trusted, interoperable, and governed knowledge networks that accelerate learning, innovation, and scientific progress while preserving organizational autonomy, privacy, and security.
