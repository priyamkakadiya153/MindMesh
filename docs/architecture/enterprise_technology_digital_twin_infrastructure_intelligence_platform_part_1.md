# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Technology Digital Twin & Infrastructure Intelligence Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin, Organizational Digital Twin, and Business Process Digital Twin, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins of enterprise technology assets, cloud environments, applications, services, infrastructure, networks, APIs, and runtime systems.

Instead of treating infrastructure as static resources, MindMesh maintains living technology twins capable of monitoring infrastructure health, predicting failures, simulating deployments, understanding dependencies, optimizing architecture, and continuously synchronizing with the operational environment.

This document defines:

* Technology Digital Twins
* Infrastructure Modeling
