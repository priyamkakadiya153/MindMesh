# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Planning Intelligence Platform (EPIP)**, the strategic planning and adaptive execution layer of the Enterprise Cognitive Operating System (ECOS).

While the Enterprise Cognitive Reasoning Fabric determines **what should be done**, the Enterprise Planning Intelligence Platform determines **how, when, by whom, and in what sequence** enterprise objectives should be achieved.

Every AI agent, executive copilot, workflow, digital twin, enterprise application, business capability, and autonomous process generates coordinated plans through a shared planning architecture.

The Enterprise Planning Platform becomes the **planning brain of the enterprise**.

To ensure compliance with the [MindMesh Engineering Constitution](file:///d:/7%20sem/MindMesh/.agents/AGENTS.md):
* **Security & Tenant Isolation**: Plan graphs, goal hierarchies, and registry objects are logically partitioned. The EPIP applies strict access verification boundaries before executing plan compilations.
* **Resilient Graceful Degradation**: Planning does not depend on AI availability. If external or local LLM planning planners are offline, the engine falls back to symbolic rules, expert systems, and deterministic constraint solving, keeping core repository organization and task sequencing functional.
* **Lineage & Explainable Strategy**: Every generated plan must log a complete trace record, detailing the reasoning steps, context vectors, and verification policies that led to the goal sequence.

# Planning Philosophy

Enterprise planning should be:

* **Goal-Oriented**: Focuses on clear organizational objectives and key results.
* **Adaptive**: Dynamically replans in response to context shifts, resource changes, and risk events.
* **Explainable**: Outputs clear steps, choices, and logic maps for every plan generation.
* **Context-Aware**: Integrates user, organizational, and regulatory context vectors.
* **Memory-Driven**: Leverages historical plans and organizational memory databases.
* **Policy-Governed**: Evaluates business guidelines, permissions, and security policies in real-time.
* **Human-Centric**: Keeps human strategy leaders in control, with AI offering advisory plans.
* **Enterprise-Wide**: Connects technical workflows, financial limits, and executive decisions.
* **Continuously Learning**: Analyzes task outcomes to optimize planning template weights.
* **AI-Native**: Structured around multi-agent collaboration and graph dependency trees.

Every enterprise objective becomes an intelligent execution plan.

# Enterprise Planning Platform

The platform consists of:

* **Planning Intelligence Engine**: The core orchestrator compiling and managing plans.
* **Goal Decomposition Engine**: The parser breaking vision priorities down into task sequences.
* **Strategic Planning Engine**: The compiler generating long-term technology, financial, and growth paths.
* **Adaptive Planning Engine**: The real-time re-planner adjusting active plans in response to events.
* **Plan Graph Engine**: The database and logic layer mapping task dependencies and resources.
* **Planning Registry**: The catalog recording active goals, plans, and templates.
* **Execution Strategy Engine**: The compiler generating specific execution routes for agents and engines.

Together these create the Enterprise Planning Intelligence Layer.
