# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Predictive Analytics & Decision Intelligence Platform that transforms historical enterprise data into predictive insights, risk intelligence, strategic recommendations, and business forecasts.

Unlike traditional BI that explains historical performance, the Predictive Intelligence Platform anticipates future outcomes and recommends optimal actions before problems occur.
