# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin, Enterprise Simulation & Autonomous Enterprise Evolution Platform** for MindMesh.

The previous phases established a complete AI-native Cognitive Enterprise with enterprise memory, reasoning, execution, learning, governance, multi-agent collaboration, and trustworthy autonomous intelligence.

Phase 13 elevates the platform by introducing a **living Cognitive Digital Twin** of the enterprise—a continuously synchronized virtual representation capable of understanding, predicting, simulating, optimizing, and autonomously evolving every aspect of the organization before changes occur in the real world.

Instead of reacting to enterprise events, MindMesh predicts future outcomes through simulation, digital experimentation, strategic modeling, and autonomous optimization.

This document defines:

* Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin
* Enterprise Simulation Architecture
* Organizational Digital Twin
* Process Digital Twin
* Technology Digital Twin
* Knowledge Digital Twin
* Strategy Simulation
* Predictive Enterprise Intelligence
* Autonomous Enterprise Evolution
* Enterprise Evolution Platform Foundation
