# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Data Digital Twin & Information Intelligence Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin, Organizational Digital Twin, Business Process Digital Twin, and Technology Digital Twin, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins of enterprise data assets, information flows, metadata, data lineage, schemas, and information ecosystems.

Instead of treating enterprise data as isolated databases, MindMesh maintains living data twins capable of understanding data relationships, tracking lineage, monitoring quality, synchronizing metadata, predicting data issues, and enabling AI-native information intelligence.

This document defines:

* Data Digital Twins
* Information Models
* Data Lineage
* Metadata Intelligence
* Data Topology
* Information Synchronization
* Data Knowledge Graph
* Information State Models
* Enterprise Information Foundation
* Information Twin Architecture
