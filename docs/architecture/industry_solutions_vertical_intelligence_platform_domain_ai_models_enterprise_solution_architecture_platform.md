# Purpose

This document defines the **MindMesh Industry Solutions Platform**, enabling the Enterprise Cognitive Operating System (ECOS) to be delivered as specialized, domain-aware solutions tailored to the needs of different industries.

Each industry solution combines domain knowledge, industry ontologies, AI models, digital twins, workflows, compliance frameworks, dashboards, and enterprise copilots into an integrated vertical platform.

Rather than delivering generic AI, MindMesh provides **industry-specific cognitive intelligence**.

To comply with the [MindMesh Engineering Constitution](file:///d:/7%20sem/MindMesh/.agents/AGENTS.md):
* **Tenant Isolation**: Industry-specific database partitions, custom ontologies, and vertical tables enforce multi-tenant schema isolation at the database level. Clinical patient data, banking ledgers, and government records can never be co-mingled or leaked across tenant lines.
* **Resilient Outage Handling**: Industry workflows include offline fallbacks. When highly specialized domain AI models (e.g. medical diagnosis assistants or financial risk analyzers) are offline, local symbolic rule-engines and cached terminology mappings sustain critical tasks without system failures.
* **Audit Tracing & Lineage**: Every vertical operation (e.g., patient record update, trade analysis, policy check) creates audit logs detailing caller context, security approvals, and information lineages.
