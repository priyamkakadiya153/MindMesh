# Purpose

This document establishes the foundational Knowledge Architecture that powers the MindMesh Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence Platform.

Knowledge is the primary asset from which intelligence is generated. Unlike traditional systems that merely store information, MindMesh transforms enterprise information into interconnected, searchable, explainable, AI-ready organizational knowledge.

This document defines:

* Enterprise Knowledge Architecture
* Enterprise Knowledge Model
* Knowledge Domains
* Knowledge Objects
* Knowledge Lifecycle
* Knowledge Engineering
* Knowledge Relationships
* Knowledge Metadata
* Knowledge Classification
* Knowledge Governance Foundations

These standards establish the structural foundation of organizational knowledge across the entire platform.
