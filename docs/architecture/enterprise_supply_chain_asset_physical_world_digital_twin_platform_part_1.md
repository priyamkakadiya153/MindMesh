# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Supply Chain, Asset & Physical World Digital Twin Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Organizational, Business Process, Technology, Data, Customer, Financial, Risk, and Sustainability Digital Twins, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins representing suppliers, logistics networks, warehouses, manufacturing facilities, inventory, transportation systems, enterprise assets, and physical infrastructure.

Rather than treating supply chain systems, ERP records, warehouse management, and IoT infrastructure as isolated operational systems, MindMesh creates living physical-world digital twins capable of continuously modeling, synchronizing, monitoring, and understanding the enterprise's physical ecosystem.

This document defines:

* Physical Asset Digital Twins
