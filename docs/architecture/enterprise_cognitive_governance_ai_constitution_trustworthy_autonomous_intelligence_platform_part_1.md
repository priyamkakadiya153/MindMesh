# Decision Boundaries

Define autonomous authority for:

### Fully Autonomous

Low-risk operational decisions.

### Supervised Autonomous

Medium-risk business decisions.

### Human Approval Required

High-risk or high-impact decisions.

### Human Exclusive

Executive, legal, regulatory, disciplinary, or strategic decisions.

Decision authority remains explicit.
