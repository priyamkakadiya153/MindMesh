# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Business Process Digital Twin & Operational Intelligence Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin and Workforce Digital Twin, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins of enterprise business processes, workflows, operational procedures, service chains, and execution models.

Rather than documenting business processes statically, MindMesh maintains living operational twins that continuously mirror real execution, predict operational outcomes, simulate workflow improvements, identify bottlenecks, and optimize enterprise operations before operational changes are deployed.

This document defines:

* Business Process Digital Twins
* Operational Models
* Workflow Simulation
* Process Intelligence
* Operational Knowledge Graph
* Process Synchronization
* Process State Models
* Operational Telemetry
* Enterprise Operations Foundation
* Operational Twin Architecture
