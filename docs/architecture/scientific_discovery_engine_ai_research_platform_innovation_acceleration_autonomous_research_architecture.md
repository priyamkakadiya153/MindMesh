# Purpose

This document presents a long-term research vision for AI-assisted scientific discovery.

It explores how future AI systems could help researchers accelerate literature review, hypothesis generation, experiment planning, data analysis, and knowledge synthesis while keeping scientific judgment, experimental validation, and publication decisions under human control.

---

# Vision

Enable researchers to discover knowledge faster by combining human expertise with AI-assisted scientific reasoning, experimentation support, and knowledge synthesis.
