# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Business Intelligence (BI) & Executive Dashboard Platform responsible for transforming governed enterprise data into actionable executive intelligence, operational visibility, KPI reporting, strategic scorecards, and decision support.

While previous documents focused on analytics infrastructure and product intelligence, this document defines how executives, department leaders, and operational teams consume trusted business intelligence through standardized dashboards and reports.

* Executive Reporting

* Business Intelligence Standards
