# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise AI Governance Architecture for MindMesh.

MindMesh is fundamentally an AI-powered enterprise platform. AI therefore requires governance equivalent to identity, security, privacy, and compliance.

This document defines:

* Enterprise AI Governance Framework
* Responsible AI Principles
* AI Risk Management
* AI Lifecycle Governance
* Model Governance
* Enterprise AI Controls
* Human Oversight
* AI Accountability
* AI Policy Framework
* AI Governance Organization

These standards ensure AI remains trustworthy, secure, explainable, compliant, and continuously governed.
