# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Financial Digital Twin & Economic Intelligence Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Organizational, Business Process, Technology, Data, and Customer Digital Twins, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins of enterprise finances, revenue streams, operational costs, budgets, investments, assets, liabilities, financial operations, and enterprise value.

Instead of maintaining disconnected accounting systems and financial reports, MindMesh creates a living financial twin capable of continuously understanding financial performance, forecasting outcomes, modeling economic scenarios, synchronizing enterprise transactions, and optimizing business value.
