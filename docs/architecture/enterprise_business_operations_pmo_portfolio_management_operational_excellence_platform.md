# Purpose

This document defines the **Enterprise Business Operations Platform**, providing the operational management system that coordinates strategy execution, portfolio governance, program delivery, operational excellence, OKRs, KPIs, resource planning, and enterprise execution.

It ensures that strategic initiatives are consistently translated into measurable business outcomes.

The Business Operations Platform becomes the **execution engine** of MindMesh.
