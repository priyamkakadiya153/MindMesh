# Purpose

This document introduces the concept of **Artificial General Enterprise Intelligence (AGEI)**—a future vision for AI systems designed specifically to understand, reason about, and support complex enterprise environments across many business domains.

Unlike general-purpose AI, AGEI is envisioned as a specialized intelligence framework focused on organizational knowledge, business processes, governance, decision support, and enterprise collaboration. It is a research direction rather than a currently available capability.

---

# Vision

Develop enterprise AI systems that continuously learn from organizational knowledge, adapt to changing business environments, and assist people with increasingly sophisticated reasoning while remaining transparent, governed, and human-directed.
