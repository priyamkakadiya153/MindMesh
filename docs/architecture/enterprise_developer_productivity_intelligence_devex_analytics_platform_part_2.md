# Vision

# Engineering Intelligence Philosophy

Engineering Intelligence should be:

* Predictive (Anticipates friction before it blocks delivery)
* Explainable (Underlying reasoning and raw metrics visible)
* Ethical (Supports developer growth, respects telemetry anonymity boundaries)
* Team-Centric (Focused on aggregate outcomes and systemic constraints)
* Privacy Preserving (Complete encryption of individual details)
* Data-Driven (Based on telemetry analytics, not subjective bias)
* Continuously Learning (Improves recommendations through feedback loops)

Insights improve engineering systems—not monitor individuals.

# Engineering Insights

Generate insights for:

* Delivery Efficiency (Identify latency stages in DORA pipelines)

# Governance

Govern:

* AI Recommendations (Ensure model outputs are explainable and unbiased)
* Productivity Analytics (Strict policy rules defining acceptable metrics use)
* Benchmark Methodology (Ensure metric definitions remain standardized across teams)
* Organizational Metrics (Secure aggregations, prevent individual evaluation misuse)
* Privacy Standards (Enforce GDPR and telemetry anonymization policies)
* AI Decision Policies (Require human approval for high-cost optimizations)

Governance maintains fairness and trust.

---

# Privacy Principles

The platform:

* Never ranks individual developers (zero developer leaderboard outputs).
* Measures teams and systems (metrics highlight systemic bottlenecks, not individual tasks).
* Uses AI responsibly (ensure recommendations improve workflow tools).
* Supports engineering growth (metrics show training and learning success).
* Protects personal data (strict RBAC encryption on commit and review logs).
* Provides explainable recommendations (all optimization tasks document metric sources).

Ethics remain foundational.

---

# Security

Protect:

* Engineering Intelligence (Restrict access to pipeline and repository telemetries)
* Benchmark Data (Enforce anonymization before aggregation storage)
* Organizational Analytics (Verify compliance with enterprise privacy guidelines)
* AI Models (Protect prompt templates and fine-tuning datasets from data leakage)
* Productivity Dashboards (Ensure scoped access configurations based on team maps)

Security aligns with Zero Trust Architecture.
