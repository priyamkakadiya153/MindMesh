# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Risk, Security & Compliance Digital Twin Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Organizational, Business Process, Technology, Data, Customer, and Financial Digital Twins, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins representing enterprise risks, cyber threats, security controls, regulatory obligations, compliance posture, governance controls, and organizational trust.

Rather than treating security and compliance as isolated operational functions, MindMesh creates living digital twins capable of continuously modeling enterprise risks, monitoring security posture, validating compliance, synchronizing controls, and enabling AI-driven trust intelligence.

This document defines:

* Risk Digital Twins
* Security Intelligence Models

* Threat Topology
