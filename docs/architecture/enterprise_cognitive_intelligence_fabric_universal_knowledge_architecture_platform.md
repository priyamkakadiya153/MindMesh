# Purpose

This document defines the **Enterprise Cognitive Intelligence Fabric (ECIF)**—the unified intelligence layer that interconnects every capability of the MindMesh Enterprise Cognitive Operating System (ECOS).

While previous phases introduced Context, Memory, Reasoning, Planning, Execution, Collaboration, Learning, Governance, and Digital Twins as individual enterprise platforms, this document integrates them into one continuously operating cognitive intelligence fabric.

The Enterprise Cognitive Intelligence Fabric becomes the **central nervous system** of the enterprise.

Every AI model, agent, workflow, application, executive copilot, digital twin, API, enterprise service, business process, and human interaction operates through this unified intelligence layer.

To comply with the [MindMesh Engineering Constitution](file:///d:/7%20sem/MindMesh/.agents/AGENTS.md):
* **Primary Objective**: The Intelligence Fabric prioritizes structuring, semantic parsing, and preservation of knowledge over conversational features.
* **Resilient Architecture**: All fabric layers downgrade to deterministic heuristics and local databases if external LLM/AI models are offline.
* **Security & Isolation**: The fabric enforces Zero Trust access boundaries, multi-tenant isolation, and complete path-lineage auditing for every resolved signal.
