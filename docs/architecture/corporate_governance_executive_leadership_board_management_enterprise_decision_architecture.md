# Purpose

This document defines the governance model for MindMesh, including board oversight, executive leadership, strategic decision-making, corporate committees, authority structures, policy governance, and executive accountability.

It ensures that every strategic, financial, operational, technological, and organizational decision aligns with the company's long-term vision.

Corporate governance becomes the **decision-making framework** of MindMesh.

---

# Vision

Establish a transparent, accountable, AI-assisted governance model that enables rapid innovation while maintaining strategic discipline, ethical leadership, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence.
