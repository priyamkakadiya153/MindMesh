# Purpose

This document explores a long-term research vision for large-scale ecosystems of autonomous AI agents that cooperate to solve complex organizational and societal problems.

Rather than functioning as isolated assistants, future AI agents may collaborate through governed communication, shared knowledge, coordinated planning, and collective problem-solving under human-defined objectives and oversight.

---

# Vision

Enable large communities of AI agents to coordinate responsibly, share knowledge, and assist people in solving challenges that exceed the capabilities of individual systems.
