# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Organizational Digital Twin & Workforce Intelligence Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Enterprise Cognitive Digital Twin introduced in Phase 13.0, this document creates a living digital representation of the organization's people, teams, skills, reporting structures, responsibilities, collaboration networks, and organizational knowledge.

The Workforce Digital Twin enables executives and AI agents to understand workforce capabilities, optimize organizational structures, forecast talent needs, simulate restructuring, identify skill gaps, and improve enterprise effectiveness before organizational changes occur.

This document defines:

* Organization Modeling
* Workforce Digital Twins
* Skills Intelligence
* Role Architecture
* Team Topologies
* Organizational Knowledge Graph
* Workforce Intelligence Foundation
* Organizational Synchronization
* Human Capability Models
* Enterprise Workforce Architecture
