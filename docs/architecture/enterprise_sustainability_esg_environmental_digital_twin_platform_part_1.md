# Purpose

This document establishes the **Enterprise Sustainability, ESG & Environmental Digital Twin Platform** for MindMesh.

Building upon the Organizational, Business Process, Technology, Data, Customer, Financial, and Risk Digital Twins, this document creates continuously synchronized digital twins representing environmental impact, ESG initiatives, carbon emissions, energy consumption, natural resources, sustainability programs, and enterprise ecological performance.

Rather than treating sustainability as isolated reporting activities, MindMesh creates living environmental digital twins capable of continuously measuring ESG performance, monitoring environmental impact, synchronizing sustainability metrics, modeling ecological scenarios, and enabling AI-driven sustainability intelligence.
