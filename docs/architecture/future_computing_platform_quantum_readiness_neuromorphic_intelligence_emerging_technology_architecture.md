# Purpose

This document explores how emerging computing technologies may influence future versions of the MindMesh platform.

It is **technology readiness guidance**, not a commitment to specific implementations. The goal is to ensure that platform architecture can evolve as new computing paradigms mature.

---

# Vision

Design enterprise architectures that remain adaptable to future advances in computing, enabling MindMesh to adopt new technologies when they become practical, reliable, secure, and commercially viable.
