# Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) formally defines the business vision, product strategy, market opportunity, target users, and long-term goals for MindMesh.

Unlike the Architecture Documents (02.x), this document focuses on **why the product exists**, **who it serves**, and **what business value it creates**.

This PRD acts as the foundation for every future feature specification and implementation guide.

---

# Executive Summary

Modern organizations use dozens of communication, documentation, and collaboration tools.

Examples include:
* Slack
* Microsoft Teams
* Discord
* Google Drive
* Notion
* Jira
* GitHub
* Confluence
* Email

Although these tools generate enormous amounts of organizational knowledge, they operate independently.

Knowledge becomes:
* fragmented
* duplicated
* difficult to search
* difficult to reuse
* quickly forgotten

Employees spend significant time searching for information instead of creating value.

MindMesh solves this problem by becoming the organization's **Knowledge Intelligence Layer**.

Instead of replacing existing tools, MindMesh connects them, understands their data using AI, and transforms fragmented information into an intelligent organizational memory.

# Problem Statement

Organizations struggle with knowledge fragmentation.

Typical problems include:

### Knowledge Silos
Information exists across multiple disconnected systems.

---

### Duplicate Work
Employees recreate documents because they cannot find previous work.

---

### Information Loss
Important conversations become impossible to locate after several weeks.

---

### Slow Onboarding
New employees spend weeks learning where information exists.

---

### Poor Search
Traditional keyword search cannot understand meaning or relationships.

---

### AI Without Context
Generic AI assistants lack organizational knowledge.

---

### Manual Knowledge Management
Employees manually organize documents, notes, and conversations.

---

### Decision Loss
Architecture decisions, meeting outcomes, and technical discussions disappear over time.

# Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of the following:
* Improve knowledge discovery
* Improve AI quality
* Reduce manual work
* Increase collaboration
* Preserve organizational memory
* Improve enterprise governance

If a feature satisfies none of these principles, it should not be built.
